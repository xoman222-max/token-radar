How a row is scored

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × chains

  • Size — TVL is the protocol today, times 0.30.
  • Growth — 7-day TVL change, clamped −50%…+100%, weighted ×0.8. A hot week helps; it cannot crown a protocol alone.
  • Maturity — 0.5 up to 1.0 over four years tracked with no token. Older with no token means more pent-up launch value.
  • Reach — +3% per extra chain, up to four. A launch on more chains reaches more holders.

“Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no token — never that the protocol has none. Implied value is this radar's own claim, not a prediction of whether or when a token launches.
